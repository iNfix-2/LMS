Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our learning model.

How are the tutors selected? expand_more
All our tutors undergo a rigorous 4-stage selection process including subject proficiency tests, demo classes, pedagogical assessments, and background checks. Only the top 3% of applicants join our platform.
Can I switch my tutor if I'm not satisfied? expand_more
Yes, student-tutor chemistry is vital. If you feel the current match isn't optimal, our student success managers will help you transition to a new tutor within 48 hours at no additional cost.
What technical equipment is required? expand_more
A stable internet connection and a device with a webcam (Laptop or Tablet) are required. For certain subjects like Math or Physics, we recommend a digital stylus or graphic tablet for real-time problem-solving on our digital whiteboard.
Do you offer trial classes? expand_more
Absolutely. We offer a complimentary 30-minute diagnostic session for every new student to assess their current level and experience our teaching methodology.
