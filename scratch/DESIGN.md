## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the academic excellence, digital accessibility, and modern learning capabilities of Edukom LMS. It leverages glassmorphic elements, rich contrast, and micro-interactions to deliver a premium, user-centric education portal.

The style is **Modern / Corporate** with a **Friendly Academic** edge. It avoids harsh lines, relying instead on clean, rounded cards, high-contrast typography, and soft shadows to establish a clear hierarchy.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from Edukom's branding guidelines:
- **Primary (Deep Blue - #002769):** Conveys trust, authority, and professionalism. Used for headers, core UI accents, and dominant action buttons.
- **Secondary / Accent (Lime Green - #71A20A):** Adds vibrancy, indicating progress, completion, success, and high-importance academic indicators.
- **Neutral Backgrounds:** Soft slate and white backgrounds to keep the focus on educational content and keep readability high.

## Typography

We employ **Plus Jakarta Sans** across the entire UI. Its geometry offers clean, modern proportions with excellent legibility on both desktop and mobile screens.
- Headings use a heavy font weight (700 or 800) with tight letter-spacing.
- Body copy uses a medium weight (400) with ample line height.

## Shapes & Roundedness

A highly rounded shape language (**16px base**) is adopted to give a modern, premium, and friendly feel to all interface cards and buttons.
- Standard cards use `16px` border-radius.
- Interactive buttons and badges use `50px` (pill-shape) for a premium tactile feel.
